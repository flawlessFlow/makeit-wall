Wall mounting

Tiles to connectors

How it goes up: assemble the wall on the floor, hold it against the wall, mark through the Ø9 mm access holes, drill, hang it back and screw through the same holes. The connectors only touch the tiles around the screw holes, so the space behind the slots stays free for hooks. Finally press the colour-matched caps into the access holes.

Print plan

How to print the tiles
  • Bambu Studio says “The 3mf file has invalid config, load geometry data only”. Click OK: parts, colours and plates are kept, print settings come from your own presets.
  • Tiles lie face down and are mirrored for that, so the artwork reads correctly once you flip the tile.
  • Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, smooth or textured PEI depending on the finish you want, outer brim, prime tower on.
  • A tile only needs the filaments that appear on it, so a 4-slot AMS can print walls with more colours tile by tile.
  • Print the colour & fit test first: your motif in miniature (160 × 120 mm) with real-size slots for your Skådis hooks, plus access holes with cover caps, an M4 hole and a snap-pin hole to check the fit.
