## Data

- **Filament colours:** the names, colour values and colour codes of Bambu Lab PLA Basic and PLA Matte come from Bambu Studio's colour list (`resources/profiles/BBL/filament/filaments_color_codes.json`, [Bambu Studio](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio), AGPL-3.0).
- **Printer and filament preset names** written into the 3MF project settings match Bambu Studio's system presets, so Bambu Studio picks the right profiles.
