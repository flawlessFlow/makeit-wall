# Changelog

## v1.0.0 – 2026-11-07

First public release.

- Multi-colour SKÅDIS-compatible pegboard walls from 80 mm to 4 m, split into tiles for Bambu Lab A1 mini, A1, P1S / P1P, X1, H2D or a custom bed
- 15 drawn examples and 15 3D wireframe shapes
- Up to 16 colours, matched to Bambu Lab PLA Basic and PLA Matte
- Cut-outs and drawn free cuts
- Optional rounded front edge on the slots
- Wall connectors with M4 screws or snap pins, colour-matched cover caps, wall gap 11–25 mm, or tiles screwed on one by one with printed spacers
- 3MF export for Bambu Studio (one plate per tile, filaments and prime tower set), connectors 3MF, assembly guide PDF
- Colour & fit test tile
- Filament shopping list, print plan, export checks
- Undo / redo and autosave in the browser
