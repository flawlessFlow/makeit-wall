# Changelog

## v2.3.0 – 2026-11-25

- All MakerWorld links (step 2 Artwork and the thank-you note after the export) now open the MakeIt Wall page on MakerWorld: https://makerworld.com/de/models/3682043-skadis-wall-art-generator-makeit-wall

## v2.2.0 – 2026-11-25

- Screw holes never overlap: two holes of one tile closer than 13 mm become one hole (e.g. a narrow strip next to a cut-out)
- Narrow strips next to cut-outs keep their full size: their corners are rounded one by one instead of the strip being cut away, and the strip gets its own hole and connector

## v2.1.0 – 2026-11-24

- Your own image as artwork: PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP, chosen, dropped onto the page or pasted. The wall takes the image's format.
- Colour around the image (when you shrink, move or turn it), with a pipette to take it from the image
- "Colours that must stay": pick colours in the image, or choose them from candidates, and they keep their place in the palette
- The image is part of undo / redo and the autosave in your browser

## v2.0.0 – 2026-11-24

- Millimetres or inches for every length on the page and in the guide PDF (button in the top bar; the first time it follows your browser's region)
- Size formats: 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, poster sizes and the IKEA SKÅDIS board sizes, with a landscape / portrait switch
- Cover caps for the screw heads, showing the artwork at their spot (the screws become one size shorter)
- "Save material": leaner print settings for the tiles – fewer back layers and 10 % infill
- Snap pins and the rounded front edge on the slots are now the defaults
- Larger, easier-to-read text on laptop screens
- The colour & fit test is upright (120 × 160 mm) for an upright wall

## v1.4.0 – 2026-11-14

- Fixed: some 3D shapes (for example Ripple) could export colour parts with open edges
- Faster 3MF export (the files are a few percent larger)

## v1.3.0 – 2026-11-07

- The link to more examples (step 2 Artwork) now opens the MakeIt Wall collection on MakerWorld

## v1.2.0 – 2026-11-07

- Link to more MakeIt Wall examples on MakerWorld (step 2 Artwork)
- Short thank-you note after the 3MF export with links to MakerWorld

## v1.0.0 – 2026-11-07

First public release.

- Multi-colour SKÅDIS-compatible pegboard walls from 80 mm to 4 m, split into tiles for Bambu Lab A1 mini, A1, P1S / P1P, X1, H2D or a custom bed
- 15 drawn examples and 15 3D wireframe shapes
- Up to 16 colours, matched to Bambu Lab PLA Basic and PLA Matte
- Cut-outs and drawn free cuts
- Optional rounded front edge on the slots
- Wall connectors with M4 screws or snap pins, colour-matched cover caps, wall gap 11–25 mm, or tiles screwed on one by one with printed spacers
- 3MF export for Bambu Studio (one plate per tile, filaments and prime tower set), connectors 3MF, assembly guide PDF
- Colour & fit test tile
- Filament shopping list, print plan, export checks
- Undo / redo and autosave in the browser
